    Recommended Battlers

  • Meatshields like Tumored Battler and Trowel Battler for general stalling. Rail Battler, when stacked, can do constant damage to Infernus from a safe distance while also obliterating smaller peons and weakening Telamon Battlers and Gehenna so your small units can take them down. As a bonus, the stage drags on for so long you can easily get more than half a dozen of these guys, and if lucky enough, you can shred through Infernus' second and third phases without much effort if you can deal with the peons.

  • Telamon Battler can soak up a bit of damage and break the armor of Gehenna, but dies easily to Treasure Seller, Uzi Rambo, or Infernus himself. Raigquitter can knockback and kill peons leaving Infernus more vulnerable and can protect your units from some of the enemies. Do be wary of the fact that although he outranges the meteors by 5, their large AOE can still hit him.

  • Micspammer Battler as it can stun all the smaller enemies in the field enough, giving you time to do what you need to do and finish. They can be killed by the meteor showers if Infernus close to Micspammer Battler, but Micspammer Battler outranges the meteors by 15 and this is unlikely, unless Infernus is in phase 3. A good chain should be able to handle most, if not all the smaller peons.
