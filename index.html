Infernus

Infernus is quite a tanky enemy, boasting high health and defense, as well as dealing a moderate amount of damage. Many of his attacks also inflict Hellfire, which can slowly cut down your defenses. Besides that, has a wide plethora of special attacks which can continue to give trouble. During his first phase, he will throw a fireball from afar which deals moderate damage plus high Hellfire damage. Infernus also has a Sword Slam that deals high damage to nearby units and summons meteors in a small area that rain down on your units and inflict Hellfire.

At 50% HP, he will transition to his second phase by stomping the ground 3 times, dealing hellfire damage while doing so, before extending the length of his blade and massively increasing his attack range. In this phase, the Meteor Showers he calls in can hit frontliners and backliners and cripple your forces, and deal even more damage than the meteors in his first phase due to the red rage passive.

Lastly, at 15% HP, Infernus will become extremely angry and stomp 4 more times, transitioning to his third phase and further enhancing his already deadly abilities. For one, his walkspeed is increased and his attack speed is doubled, increasing his pushing power to insane levels. Second, he unleashes a hellfire aura that constantly applies Hellfire on any battlers that enter its range in a large area. The Meteor Shower from his second phase will also become permanently active until he dies, but the activated ability has been replaced with his Sword Slam again.

Once Infernus dies, he will fall onto the ground, start crying and slam the ground multiple times, creating 6 shockwaves that deal massive damage to nearby units, before finally dying by being struck with a lightning.
